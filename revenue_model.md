## 1. Revenue Model Overview

### Business Model: SaaS Subscription + Usage Add-ons

```
Revenue Streams
├── 1. Subscription Revenue (70-80% of total)
│   ├── Pro         €19/mo | €182/yr
│   ├── Pro+        €39/mo | €374/yr  ← Core revenue driver
│   ├── Elite       €79/mo | €758/yr
│   └── Enterprise  €149/mo | €1,430/yr
├── 2. Add-on Revenue (10-15%)
│   ├── AI Pack 500    €9.90/mo
│   ├── AI Pack 1000   €14.90/mo
│   ├── Progetti ×10   €9.90/mo
│   └── Storage 5/20GB €2.90-7.90/mo
├── 3. Expansion Revenue (5-10%)
│   └── Upgrade path: Pro → Pro+ → Elite → Enterprise
└── 4. Future Revenue (roadmap)
    ├── Transaction fees (payment processing)
    ├── White-label licensing
    ├── Marketplace commissions
    └── Referral/partner commissions
```
